Finding 01 · Where the money goes

Five demands dominate the budget

In 2024-25, Karnataka actually spent ₹368,397 crore — 99.4% of the ₹370,658 crore it budgeted. These five demands account for most of it.

Finding 03 · The structural squeeze

A quarter of spending is committed

Salaries, pensions, and interest are committed expenditure — hard to cut. Their share of total spending rose from 23% in 2014-15 to 25% in 2024-25.

The fastest-growing demands

How these numbers were made

Auditable, not just asserted

Every figure here is summed only from additive leaf rows (type_of_table = object_head, row_type = Data, row_level = Object-Head) — totals and summary rows are excluded, so nothing is double-counted. The four analyses are implemented three times, in Python, R, and DuckDB, and must agree to the paisa.
